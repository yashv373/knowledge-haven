## About Me

Hi, I'm **Yashvardhan Singh**. 

I am an EE-VLSI undergraduate at **MIT Manipal** (Class of 2027) and currently a Digital IP Platform Design Intern at **STMicroelectronics** in Greater Noida, working within the MDRF group on the STM Stellar-X architecture. 

My world revolves around hardware acceleration, digital ASIC design, and open-source silicon. I co-founded and served as President of **ChipMIT** (India's first undergraduate chip design club), where we executed projects like *KALKI*, an RTL-to-GDSII implementation of a 4x4 Systolic Array MAC Accelerator. My research background involves exploring spintronics-based ASICs and analyzing CMOS-MTJ sense amplifiers for hybrid Compute-in-Memory architectures (published in the *IEEE JxCDC* journal). 

## The Purpose of The Silicon Drafts

This site serves as my personal, living notebook. It's a raw, unfiltered log of my journey through Digital Design, Computer Architecture, and Hardware Engineering. You'll find my study notes, RTL practice logs (such as my progression through the Harris & Harris DDCA textbook), and insights from building custom hardware accelerators.
